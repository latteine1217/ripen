# Code of Conduct

Ripen follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/latteine1217/ripen/security/advisories/new) or by contacting [@latteine1217](https://github.com/latteine1217). Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove content or block participants who violate this code.
