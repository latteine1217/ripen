# Security Policy

## Supported versions

Only the [latest release](https://github.com/latteine1217/ripen/releases/latest) receives fixes. Use **Check for Updates…** in the Ripen menu to stay current.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue. Report it privately through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/latteine1217/ripen/security/advisories/new) with a description, the affected version and steps to reproduce.

You can expect an acknowledgement within 7 days. Once a fix is released, the advisory is published with credit to the reporter unless you prefer otherwise.

## Scope

Ripen has no account and stores focus history only in `~/Library/Application Support/Ripen`. It goes online only when you choose **Check for Updates…**, which downloads a release from GitHub, verifies it against GitHub's SHA-256 checksum, bundle identifier, version and signature, and installs it after you confirm. Reports about that update path, local data handling, or anything that lets another process tamper with Ripen are especially welcome.
